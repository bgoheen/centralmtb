01 Our Crew

A supportive crew on two wheels.

We ride the Twin Cities' best trails - Lebanon Hills, Battle Creek, Theo Wirth, Carver, Sunfish - and a bunch in between. Our coaches teach skills, build confidence, and keep the energy loose. We're a team, not a tryout.

New to mountain biking? Perfect. We'll teach you everything from balance to berms. Earn a varsity letter, race if you want to (totally optional), and finish the season with a crew that gets you.

04 Asked & Answered

Common questions.

What skill level do I need to join? +
None. Seriously. We welcome riders of every skill level, from people who haven't been on a bike since elementary school to riders who already race. Coaches split practices into ability groups so you ride with people at your pace.
What kind of bike and gear do I need? +
A mountain bike (hardtail or full suspension), a helmet (MIPS recommended), and basic gear like gloves and a water bottle. Check the equipment guide for specifics, and contact us if you need help finding affordable options - we know the local shops and the used market.
How big is the time commitment? +
Three practices a week - Tuesday and Thursday evenings (5:30–7:30 pm) and Saturday mornings (9–11 am) - at Twin Cities trails. The season runs from the first week of July through mid-October. Participation is flexible; we know summer plans happen.
Do I have to race? +
Nope. Racing is completely optional. A lot of our riders focus on trail rides and skill-building and never pin on a number. Others go after every race they can. You decide what fits.
Which schools can join the team? +
Grades 6–12 from St. Paul Central, Harding, Johnson, Como Park, and most other St. Paul schools. Not sure where you land? Drop us a line.
What does it cost to join? +
Team dues are $90 per rider plus a $125 league registration fee. Racing fees are separate and optional. The cost breakdown above covers everything end-to-end, including gear estimates.

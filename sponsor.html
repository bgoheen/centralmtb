Become a Sponsor

Help us keep more kids on bikes.

The St. Paul Central Mountain Bike Team is a volunteer-run program serving roughly 50 student-athletes in grades 6 through 12 across the St. Paul school district. We are not funded by the school. Every dollar that keeps this team running comes from families, community partners, and sponsors like you.

Your sponsorship reaches far beyond the riders. Parents, siblings, and volunteers show up every week at practices and at races across the metro all season long. When your name is on our banner, hundreds of people see it, and they see a business that invests in kids.

This is not a donation. It is a partnership. You help us cover the real costs of running a youth cycling team, and we put your business in front of an engaged, community-minded audience.

Where Your Sponsorship Goes

Race Day Food and Supplies

Feeding riders and volunteers at all-day race events throughout the season.

Tools and Bike Maintenance

Trail-side repair kits, spare tubes, pumps, and workshop supplies to keep riders rolling.

Team Loaner Bikes

Purchasing bikes so that any student can ride regardless of what they own.

Rider Scholarships

Covering registration fees so cost is never the reason a kid misses the season.
